## Overview

Dograh AI answers voice calls that WhatsApp users place to your WhatsApp Business number. Only inbound calls are supported.

## Prerequisites

- A Meta business portfolio with a WhatsApp Business phone number on Cloud API
- Dograh AI reachable from the internet over HTTPS

<Warning>
  Follow the steps in order. Meta verifies the webhook against a saved Dograh
  configuration, and won't turn on calling until a `calls` webhook exists.
</Warning>

## Step 1: Create a Meta App

1. Go to [developers.facebook.com/apps](https://developers.facebook.com/apps) and click **Create App**
2. Enter an app name and contact email, then click **Next**
3. Select **Connect with customers through WhatsApp** and click **Next**
4. Select the business portfolio that owns your WhatsApp number, click **Next**, then click **Create app**

## Step 2: Collect Credentials

**WhatsApp Business account ID**

1. In your app, open **Use cases** → **Connect on WhatsApp** → **API Setup**
2. Select any of your numbers in the **From** dropdown
3. Copy the **WhatsApp Business account ID** shown below it. One Dograh configuration covers every number on the account.

**Access token**

1. Open [Business settings](https://business.facebook.com/settings) → **Users** → **System users** and select a system user with **Admin** access (click **Add** to create one)
2. Click **⋯** → **Assign assets**, and give the system user full control of your app and your WhatsApp account
3. Click **Generate token**, select your app, set expiration to **Never**, and grant `whatsapp_business_messaging` and `whatsapp_business_management`
4. Copy the token — Meta shows it only once

**App secret**

1. In your app, open **App settings** → **Basic**
2. Click **Show** next to **App secret** and copy it

## Step 3: Configure in Dograh AI

1. Navigate to **/telephony-configurations**, click **Add configuration**, and select **WhatsApp**
2. Enter the **Access Token**, **WhatsApp Business Account ID**, and **App Secret**, then click **Create**. Dograh checks them with Meta and subscribes your app to the account's webhooks before saving.
3. Open the new configuration. Under **Webhook for your Meta app**, copy the **Callback URL** and the **Verify token** — you'll paste both into Meta in Step 4
4. In **Phone numbers**, click **Refresh numbers**. Dograh imports the numbers Meta lists on the account. The result names any number it had to skip, so check it before assigning workflows.
5. Edit each number that should take calls and set its **Inbound workflow** to the agent that should answer. A number with no workflow rejects calls.

<Note>
  On self-hosted Dograh, the Callback URL must be your backend's public HTTPS
  URL. If the page shows a `localhost` address, expose your backend (for
  example with a tunnel) and use that hostname instead.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Added a number to the account in Meta later? Open the configuration and click
  **Refresh numbers** again. Numbers Meta no longer lists stay in Dograh and are
  reported in the result so you can remove them yourself.
</Tip>

## Step 4: Configure the Webhook in Meta

1. In your app, open **Use cases** → **Connect on WhatsApp** → **Configuration**
2. Paste the **Callback URL** and **Verify token** from Step 3, then click **Verify and save**
3. In the **Webhook fields** list that appears, turn on **Subscribe** for `calls`

## Step 5: Turn On Calling

1. Open [WhatsApp Manager](https://business.facebook.com/latest/whatsapp_manager/) → **Account tools** → **Phone numbers**
2. Click the gear icon next to each number and open **Call settings**
3. Turn on **Allow voice calls**

Alternatively, turn on calling via the Graph API (useful when testing with a Meta test phone number):

```bash
curl -X POST "https://graph.facebook.com/v26.0/<PHONE_NUMBER_ID>/settings" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <ACCESS_TOKEN>" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "calling": {
      "status": "ENABLED",
      "call_icon_visibility": "DEFAULT"
    }
  }'
```

A successful call returns `{"success": true}`.

## Step 6: Test

Call one of your business numbers from the WhatsApp app on a phone. Your Dograh AI agent answers.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="'This business is not accepting calls'">
    Calling is off for the number. Complete Step 5.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="'To use WhatsApp calling features, you need to configure webhooks or set up SIP'">
    The app has no `calls` webhook yet. Complete Step 4, then turn on calling.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Create fails with 'Meta did not accept these credentials'">
    Meta could not list the account's numbers with the access token. Check the
    WhatsApp Business Account ID against the API Setup page, and that the
    token's system user has full control of your WhatsApp account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Create fails with 'Meta did not let this app subscribe'">
    The token's system user needs `whatsapp_business_management` and full
    control of the WhatsApp account. Regenerate the token with that permission
    and save again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Verify and save fails">
    Save the Dograh configuration first, and make sure the Verify token in
    Meta matches the one on the configuration page exactly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Refresh numbers finds nothing, or misses a number">
    Dograh lists the numbers Meta shows under the WhatsApp Business Account ID
    you saved. Check that the number is on that account in WhatsApp Manager,
    and that the access token's system user has full control of it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
