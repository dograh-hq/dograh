## Overview

Dograh AI answers voice calls that WhatsApp users place to your WhatsApp Business number. Only inbound calls are supported.

## Prerequisites

- A Meta business portfolio with a WhatsApp Business phone number on Cloud API
- Dograh AI reachable from the internet over HTTPS

<Warning>
  Follow the steps in order. Meta verifies the webhook against a saved Dograh
  configuration, and won't turn on calling until a `calls` webhook exists.
</Warning>

## Step 1: Create a Meta App

1. Go to [developers.facebook.com/apps](https://developers.facebook.com/apps) and click **Create App**
2. Enter an app name and contact email, then click **Next**
3. Select **Connect with customers through WhatsApp** and click **Next**
4. Select the business portfolio that owns your WhatsApp number, click **Next**, then click **Create app**

## Step 2: Collect Credentials

**Phone Number ID and WhatsApp Business account ID**

1. In your app, open **Use cases** → **Connect on WhatsApp** → **API Setup**
2. Select your number in the **From** dropdown
3. Copy the **Phone number ID** and the **WhatsApp Business account ID** shown below it

**Access token**

1. Open [Business settings](https://business.facebook.com/settings) → **Users** → **System users** and select a system user with **Admin** access (click **Add** to create one)
2. Click **⋯** → **Assign assets**, and give the system user full control of your app and your WhatsApp account
3. Click **Generate token**, select your app, set expiration to **Never**, and grant `whatsapp_business_messaging` and `whatsapp_business_management`
4. Copy the token — Meta shows it only once

**App secret**

1. In your app, open **App settings** → **Basic**
2. Click **Show** next to **App secret** and copy it

## Step 3: Configure in Dograh AI

1. Navigate to **/telephony-configurations**, click **Add configuration**, and select **WhatsApp**
2. Enter the **Access Token**, **Phone Number ID**, and **App Secret**, then click **Create**. Dograh checks them with Meta before saving.
3. Open the new configuration. Under **Webhook for your Meta app**, copy the **Callback URL** and the **Verify token** — you'll paste both into Meta in Step 5
4. Click **Add phone number**, enter your WhatsApp Business number with its country code (for example `+15551234567`), set the **Inbound workflow** to the agent that should answer, and click **Add**. Dograh checks the number against Meta before saving it.

<Note>
  On self-hosted Dograh, the Callback URL must be your backend's public HTTPS
  URL. If the page shows a `localhost` address, expose your backend (for
  example with a tunnel) and use that hostname instead.
</Note>

## Step 4: Subscribe Your App to the WhatsApp Business Account

```bash
curl -X POST "https://graph.facebook.com/v26.0/<WHATSAPP_BUSINESS_ACCOUNT_ID>/subscribed_apps" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <ACCESS_TOKEN>"
```

A successful call returns `{"success": true}`.

## Step 5: Configure the Webhook in Meta

1. In your app, open **Use cases** → **Connect on WhatsApp** → **Configuration**
2. Paste the **Callback URL** and **Verify token** from Step 3, then click **Verify and save**
3. In the **Webhook fields** list that appears, turn on **Subscribe** for `calls`

## Step 6: Turn On Calling

1. Open [WhatsApp Manager](https://business.facebook.com/latest/whatsapp_manager/) → **Account tools** → **Phone numbers**
2. Click the gear icon next to your number and open **Call settings**
3. Turn on **Allow voice calls**

## Step 7: Test

Call your business number from the WhatsApp app on a phone. Your Dograh AI agent answers.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="'This business is not accepting calls'">
    Calling is off for the number. Complete Step 6.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="'To use WhatsApp calling features, you need to configure webhooks or set up SIP'">
    The app has no `calls` webhook yet. Complete Step 5, then turn on calling.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Create fails with 'Meta did not accept these credentials'">
    Meta could not read the Phone Number ID with the access token. Check the
    Phone Number ID against the API Setup page, and that the token's system
    user has full control of your WhatsApp account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Verify and save fails">
    Save the Dograh configuration first, and make sure the Verify token in
    Meta matches the one on the configuration page exactly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Add phone number is rejected">
    Dograh asks Meta which number sits behind your Phone Number ID and only
    accepts that one. Check that the number you typed matches the number shown
    on the API Setup page, and that the access token's system user has full
    control of your WhatsApp account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
